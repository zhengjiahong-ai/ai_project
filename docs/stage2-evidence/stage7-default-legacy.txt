In this paper, we investigate an active RIS-assisted integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) system in which the BS senses the target in the presence of interference and establishes communication with multiple users assisted by the RIS. The optimization problem is formulated to jointly design the BS’s sensing transceiver beamforming, communication beamforming, and the RIS’s reflection coefficients to maximize the sensing SINR while satisfying the communication SINR requirements of users. An algorithm based on the ideas of quadratic transformation (QT), Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) conditions, semidefinite relaxation (SDR), and alternating opti- mization (AO) is designed to transform and solve the non-convex problem optimally. Simulation results verify the effectiveness and superiority of active RIS-assisted ISAC systems in improving sensing performance with communication SINR constraints. Index Terms —Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), reconfigurable intelligent surface (RIS), transmit and receive beamforming design. I. I NTRODUCTION Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) and recon- figurable intelligent surface (RIS) are two promising tech- nologies with significant implications for future autonomous driving and the sixth generation (6G) wireless communication systems. ISAC technology allows communication and radar systems to share spectrum resources, offering high spectral efficiency and low costs, making it a potential technology for Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) [1]. RIS is composed of many passive reflection elements that can dynamically adjust the phase of incident signals, establishing a reliable virtual link between the base station (BS) and users, making it a significant research hotspot. Due to the advantages of RIS, there have been many research studies exploring the deployment of active RIS in ISAC systems [2]–[4]. The work [2] used RIS to assist multi-user communication and target sensing, maximizing the sum rate of communication users and meeting the worst-case This work is supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China (Grant numbers 62371406, 62171392, 61971365). *Corresponding author: Yifeng Zhao. sensing signal-to-noise ratio (SNR). The work [3] aimed to maximize the weighted summation of the target sensing SNR under each user’s communication quality of service (QoS) requirement. The work [4] has taken into account the presence of interference sources around the target. It maximizes the sensing signal-to-interference-and-noise-ratio (SINR) while minimizing multi-user communication interference through a joint waveform and transmit beamforming design. However, although the above work considered the existence of sensing interference in realistic scenarios, it still faces the challenge of multiplicative fading associated with passive RIS used for communication assistance [5]. Active RIS equipped with active loads on reflection elements has been proposed to reflect and amplify signals, thereby enhancing system performance [6]. Therefore, deploying active RIS in ISAC systems with sensing interference is a promising strategy. In addition, in order to better meet the needs of future intelligent traffic scenarios [7], we further study the joint design of sensing transmit and receive beamforming in the RIS-assisted ISAC system [8]. Motivated by the discussions mentioned above, in this paper, we investigate an active RIS-assisted ISAC system. The ISAC BS senses the target of interest (such as vehi- cles, unmanned aerial vehicles, and pedestrians) with nearby sources of interference. Simultaneously, due to obstructed line- of-sight (LoS) links between the BS and users, the BS provides communication services for multiple users with the assistance of active RIS. The remainder of this article unfolds as follows: Section II presents the system model and the formulated problem. In Section III, an algorithm for transforming and solving the problem is proposed. Section IV presents the numerical results. Finally, Section V concludes this paper. II. Sensing SINR versus system power budget P total ( γ th = 5dB ). V. C ONCLUSION This paper studies an active RIS-assisted ISAC system with sensing interference sources. The objective is to maximize the sensing SINR while satisfying communication SINR con- straints, the transmit power constraints of BS and active RIS, and unit-modulus constraints for receive beamforming. An algorithm based on QT, KKT, and SDR methods is proposed to transform the non-convex problem into several subproblems, which are then iteratively solved. Numerical results show that under the same power budget, deploying active RIS is a better strategy than both passive and random RIS schemes.
