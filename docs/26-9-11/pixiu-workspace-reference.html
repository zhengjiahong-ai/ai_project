Chain-of-Thought Reasoning in Large Language Models

3   Method

In this section, we introduce chain-of-thought prompting, which encourages large language models to generate intermediate reasoning steps before producing the final answer. We first describe the overall framework, followed by the details of prompt construction, model training, and inference strategy.

3.1   Chain-of-Thought Prompting

Chain-of-thought (CoT) prompting elicits a series of intermediate natural language reasoning steps from large language models, which significantly improves performance on complex reasoning tasks. Instead of directly generating the final answer, the model is encouraged to “think step by step”.

Formally, given a question, we construct a prompt that includes input-reasoning-answer demonstrations followed by the final answer. This inference-time approach leverages the in-context learning capability of LLMs and works remarkably well across a wide range of tasks, including arithmetic, commonsense, and symbolic reasoning.

3.2   Model Training

Chain-of-thought prompting can be applied to both pre-trained and instruction-tuned models without additional training. We use few-shot demonstrations sampled from task-specific training sets, where each example contains a natural language reasoning process and the corresponding final answer.
